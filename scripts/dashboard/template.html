Reference-Free Coil Benchmark

Four data sets, one question: where do the Biot–Savart PINN's coil maps stand against NLINV and ESPIRiT? Static slices from your dataloader (fastMRI knee and brain, Calgary-Campinas) at R = 4 with 24 ACS lines; fully sampled OCMR cardiac cines with GRO undersampling at R = 4 and 8; prospectively undersampled OCMR cines (R ≈ 9); and the MPRAGE brain planes of the phase-pole paper with tiny calibration regions. Every method sees the same samples. Maps are scored against fully sampled data where there is any, and on held-out k-space where there is none.

{{CAVEATS}}
Definitions

Methods

PINN v53
estimate_maps(solve_image=True, optimizer="adam+lbfgs") as validated in the joint evaluation: calibration fit, then maps and image jointly on all samples.
PINN v54 (default)
v53 plus the smooth-factor and coupling penalties in the joint stage.
PINN v56 / v55
v54 plus support from the fully sampled ACS block, an L-BFGS joint finish with variable projection and a zero-mean smooth factor. Off by default since v56b.
PINN, time-averaged
2-D maps from the time-averaged k-space of a cine, used for every frame.
PINN dynamic
layout="dynamic": a static base from the time average plus low-rank per-frame corrections (rank 0 = base only).
NLINV + pole correction
BART nlinv --phase-pole 0 (BART 1.0), the paper's correction.
Leave-coil-out / floor
Coil j predicted from the other coils with the maps, against what pure noise leaves through the same operator; 1 = as good as the true maps. Gauge-invariant.
Gauge pole
A point around which most coils' phases wind the same way, also over loops of radius 6 pixels (NLINV's poles are spread).
